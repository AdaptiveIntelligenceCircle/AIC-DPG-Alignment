## Review principle

Every addition must pass these tests:

1. Does it increase or decrease the risk of over-claiming?
2. Does it make limitations clearer?
3. Does it remain secondary to the actual technical and governance work of AIC?

If the answer to the first question is unfavorable, the change should be rejected.
