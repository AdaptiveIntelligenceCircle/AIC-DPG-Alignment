# SDG Relevance

## Purpose

This document explains how AIC-DPG-Alignment may relate to selected United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

The repository does not claim direct achievement of any SDG target.

Instead, it explores how governance-oriented documentation, transparency practices, and public-interest infrastructure thinking may indirectly support SDG-related efforts.

---

## SDG 4 – Quality Education

Potential contributions:

- Open educational resources.
- Governance literacy.
- Public documentation.
- Transparent knowledge sharing.

Potential limitations:

- No direct educational service is provided.
- No formal curriculum is maintained.

---

## SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Potential contributions:

- Open governance frameworks.
- Documentation supporting public-interest infrastructure.
- Reusable assessment templates.

Potential limitations:

- The repository is not infrastructure itself.
- No operational digital infrastructure is deployed.

---

## SDG 16 – Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Potential contributions:

- Documentation practices.
- Institutional memory.
- Transparency principles.
- Accountability-oriented templates.
- Governance discussions.

Potential limitations:

- No legal authority.
- No regulatory capability.
- No dispute resolution authority.

---

## SDG 17 – Partnerships for the Goals

Potential contributions:

- Cross-organizational collaboration.
- Shared governance discussions.
- Open review models.

Potential limitations:

- Partnership outcomes depend on participating entities.
- No partnership guarantees exist.

---

## Under-Claim Statement

References to SDGs should not be interpreted as evidence of SDG achievement.

This repository only provides conceptual and documentation-level alignment.
