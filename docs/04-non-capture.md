# 04 – Non-Capture

## Core idea

Non-capture is a central design goal of AIC (expressed through Third Path and the Zero-donation spirit).

The project aims to reduce the likelihood that the protocol family, its governance, or its public materials become instruments of control by:

- Large funders
- Political agendas
- Commercial interests seeking rule-power
- Any single institutional actor

## Implications for this repository

- Documentation must not be written in ways that increase capture risk.
- Claims of alignment with external frameworks (including DPG-related ones) must remain careful and reversible.
- No part of this repository should be interpretable as granting special status or legitimacy that could later be leveraged for control.

Non-capture is treated as an ongoing responsibility, not a one-time achievement.
