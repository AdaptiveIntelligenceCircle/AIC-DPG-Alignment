# 03 – Do No Harm

## Statement

Adaptive Intelligence Circle treats “Do No Harm” as a design constraint, not a slogan.

In the context of this repository and the broader project:

- AIC materials and tools should not facilitate hidden control by funders, states, or commercial entities.
- AIC should not encourage the replacement of human ethical responsibility with automated systems.
- AIC should not create new dependencies that reduce the independence of organizations or individuals who engage with it.
- Public statements should not create false expectations about readiness, safety, or impact.

## Practical implications

- Prefer fail-closed behavior where authorization or high-stakes decisions are involved.
- Keep human judgment central (Human Meaning / NeedHuman).
- Maintain under-claim language in all public materials.
- Document limitations clearly rather than minimizing them.

This document records intent. Realization depends on ongoing technical and governance work.
