# Relation to Other AIC Repositories

## Purpose

This repository is part of the broader AIC ecosystem.

It should be viewed as an alignment and governance-support repository rather than an operational infrastructure repository.

---

## Relationship with AIC Whitepaper

The Whitepaper defines high-level vision, principles, and institutional objectives.

AIC-DPG-Alignment explores how these ideas may relate to:

- DPG standards
- Public-interest infrastructure
- Governance principles
- SDG relevance

---

## Relationship with The Third Path

The Third Path provides:

- Boundaries
- Trade-offs
- Falsifiable assumptions
- Institutional constraints

This repository applies some of those concepts in the context of DPG alignment.

---

## Relationship with AIC-Tools-Policy

AIC-Tools-Policy focuses on:

AIC-DPG-Alignment focuses on:
