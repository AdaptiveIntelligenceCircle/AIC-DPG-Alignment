# 01 – Purpose and Scope

## Purpose

This repository documents the relationship between Adaptive Intelligence Circle (AIC) and the concepts commonly discussed under the umbrella of Digital Public Goods (DPGs).

It exists to support transparency, especially in the context of AIC’s submission of materials related to the Digital Public Goods Alliance review process.

## Scope

In scope:
- Mapping of existing AIC principles to DPG-related expectations
- Explicit statements on Do No Harm and Non-Capture
- Modest discussion of possible relevance to Sustainable Development Goals
- Clear recording of limitations and under-claim posture

Out of scope:
- Formal certification claims
- Quantitative impact assessment
- Technical implementation details of Parallel TestNet
- Legal opinions or compliance guarantees

## Intended audience

- AIC contributors and maintainers
- External reviewers who want to understand AIC’s self-positioning
- Organizations exploring whether AIC principles are relevant to their own work

This is orientation and documentation material, not a product or service.
