# AIC-DPG-Alignment

**Documentation of how Adaptive Intelligence Circle relates to Digital Public Goods concepts, the DPG Standard, and related principles.**

> This is not a certification.  
> This is not a claim of compliance.  
> This is a careful, under-claim record of alignment efforts and limitations.

**Status:** Early documentation (November 2026)  
**Parent project:** [Adaptive Intelligence Circle](https://github.com/AdaptiveIntelligenceCircle)  
**Context:** AIC has submitted materials related to the Digital Public Goods Alliance (DPGA) review process. This repository exists to make the reasoning transparent.

---

## Purpose

AIC-DPG-Alignment exists to:

- Record how core AIC principles relate to the ideas commonly associated with Digital Public Goods
- Make explicit the areas of potential alignment as well as the clear limitations
- Support internal and external review with inspectable documentation
- Prevent over-claiming about readiness, impact, or official status

It is **not** intended to market AIC as a certified Digital Public Good, nor to create expectations that the current state of the project already meets all DPG criteria.
